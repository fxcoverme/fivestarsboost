FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Who pays for the ads?

You do — directly to the platform (Google, Meta, or TikTok), from your own ad account and your own card. Our management fee is separate. The two never mix.

Do you guarantee results?

No — and be careful with anyone who does. Ad performance depends on your market, your budget, your competition, and your reviews. What we guarantee is the work: careful setup, weekly attention, wasted spend cut, and an honest monthly account of what your money did.

How much ad budget do I need?

For the pilot we recommend $500/month to start. That's enough to learn what works in most local markets without risking real money finding out.

What happens if I cancel?

Give us 30 days' notice. Your Ads account, campaigns, and data stay with you — they were always yours. We remove our manager access and hand over a summary of what was running and what we'd learned.

Why combine ads with review management?

Because the click is only half the job. A customer who finds your ad will almost always look at your reviews before calling. Ads decide whether they find you; reviews decide whether they choose you. We make sure both answers are yes.

What do you need from me to start?

A Google account for your business, 15 minutes to set up your Ads account and add your payment method, and your answers to three questions: what services to advertise, what area you serve, and what hours you answer the phone. We handle the rest.
