Instagram Ads for Salons: Clicks Are Easy — Bookings Take Reviews

Instagram ads for salons are the most natural fit in local advertising: the work is visual, the before-and-after sells itself, and your next client is already scrolling. But the tap on your ad is the beginning of her decision, not the end — the booking happens after she checks your Google reviews.

Which platform fits a salon

Instagram and Facebook (Meta) lead for salons: transformation video and photos, targeted by neighborhood, age, and interest, sent to a booking link. Google is the second act — “balayage near me” and “nail salon open Sunday” are typed by people ready to book this week, and your stars appear beside the result. TikTok runs on the same visual logic with a younger skew; it suits stylists building a personal book. For most salons: Meta for discovery, Google for the ready-to-book — and reviews underneath both, because every platform's click ends with the same search of your name.

Running it yourself vs having it managed

Boosting your best post reaches people who like looking at hair — not necessarily people within three miles who need a cut this week. Managed campaigns target by radius and service, send clicks to booking instead of to your grid, and track calls. The quieter DIY leak is staleness: ads still running for a stylist who left in spring, or a service you no longer offer, because nobody has touched the campaign since it launched.
