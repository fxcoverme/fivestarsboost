Google first, and it isn't close

Breakdowns and maintenance are searched with intent, and map placement puts your shop in front of drivers within a few miles at the exact moment of need. Meta has a supporting role — maintenance reminders and seasonal offers shown to people who already know the shop. TikTok is a weak fit for repair decisions; it can show shop personality, but nobody scrolls their way into a transmission job. Google first — add a second platform only when the first is earning its keep.

Running it yourself vs having it managed

Shop owners are on the lift all day, and DIY campaigns show it: months without pruning while the budget leaks into searches for parts, do-it-yourself repair videos, dealerships, and jobs — plus clicks from drivers far outside any realistic drive distance. Managed campaigns are built by service (brakes, diagnostics, transmission), held to a tight radius, scheduled for the hours the shop answers the phone, and tracked by calls. The weekly search-term review is the whole difference: wasted spend gets found and cut week after week, instead of at the end of a quarter.
