A case we measured

A med spa with 128 Google reviews and a strong overall rating. Of its 10 most recent reviews, 8 had no reply at all — including a lukewarm 3-star review questioning whether a treatment was worth the price. The treatment rooms were booked and the results were good; the public conversation about value had simply been left for strangers to finish on their own.

For high-ticket services, an unanswered question about price does not stay neutral. It becomes the answer.
