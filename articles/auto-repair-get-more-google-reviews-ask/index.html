Get More Google Reviews: The Auto Repair Ask That Follows a Good Job

The typical repair shop has 40 Google reviews. Map leaders have 188. To get more Google reviews, a shop does not need a campaign. It needs a simple ask at the right moment — at pickup, when the car is fixed and the relief is real — and a reply to every review that follows.

A case we measured

An independent repair shop with 58 Google reviews and a 4.7-star rating. Of its 10 most recent reviews, 5 had no reply at all. Customers were happy — several reviews named the technician — but nobody thanked them in public and nobody asked the next driver. The shop was doing the work that earns reviews and skipping the two small steps that collect them.

A reply costs little and changes the story. A good answer is two sentences:

""Thank you for trusting us with your car — we're glad the repair was explained clearly and finished on time. We appreciate you telling other drivers about it here.""

A named, grateful thank-you is the reply that makes the next customer comfortable leaving one too.
