How to Respond to Restaurant Reviews: Examples That Protect the Next Table

Only 9.8% of 1-star reviews ever get a reply. Learning how to respond to restaurant reviews — with a real example, not a template — is what turns a bad night into evidence that the next guest will be looked after.

Restaurants report replying to about 60% of reviews, yet the 1-star reviews still slip through. Restaurant review management is not about winning an argument in public. Google reviews for restaurants are read by the next table, not the last one — so each answer should name what happened, say what changed, and invite the guest back to see it.

A case we measured

An independent restaurant with 287 Google reviews and a 4.3-star rating. Of its 10 most recent reviews, 5 had no reply at all — including a 1-star review about a long wait on a busy Friday, unanswered for twelve days. The kitchen had been short-staffed that night. The review page never explained, apologised, or showed what was fixed.

A reply costs little and changes the story. A good answer is two sentences:

""We're sorry Friday's wait was far too long — we were short-staffed and should have told you at the door. We've added a host on Fridays to keep waits honest. Please come back and let us make it right.""

Specific, short, and forward-looking — and the next guest sees a restaurant that fixes problems instead of hiding from them.
