Online Reputation Management for Home Service Businesses: Protecting Leads You Already Paid For

Contractors already pay $50–230 for every lead. Online reputation management is how you stop losing the ones that reach your door — because 91% of consumers read your reviews before they ever call.

A case we measured

An HVAC company with 34 Google reviews and a 4.2-star rating. Of its 10 most recent reviews, 8 had no reply — including a 1-star review about a no-show appointment during a heat wave, still unanswered a month later. The office had rebooked the customer the same day. Every homeowner who searched during the next heat wave saw only the no-show story.

A reply costs little and changes the story. To that review, a good answer is two sentences:

"We're sorry we missed your appointment during the heat wave — that's not the reliability we promise. We've tightened our dispatch confirmations, and we'd welcome the chance to earn your trust back."

Accountable and specific — the next homeowner reads that as a company that shows up, on the page and on the job.
