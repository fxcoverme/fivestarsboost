Review Monitoring for Med Spas: Catch Every Review on a $527 Decision

Businesses reply to only 46.9% of their Google reviews on average. Review monitoring is what closes that gap — and at an average visit value of $527, a med spa cannot afford to learn about a bad review from the next client.

What the data says about medical spa reviews

Aesthetic practice reviews are read slowly and skeptically: prospective clients compare before-and-after stories, then check how the practice answers criticism. A med spa reputation is decided in that second step — the reply, or the silence where a reply should be.

A case we measured

A med spa with 88 Google reviews and a 4.5-star rating. Of its 10 most recent reviews, 7 had no reply — including a detailed complaint about post-treatment redness that had waited three weeks for any response. The clinical follow-up had happened by phone. Publicly, the next ten consultation shoppers saw only the complaint.

A reply costs little and changes the story. To that review, a good answer is two sentences:

"Thank you for sharing this — some redness can be expected, but you should never feel unsure about your aftercare. Please contact our office so our clinical team can check in with you directly."

Careful, compliant, and visibly attentive — exactly what a nervous first-time client needs to see before booking.
