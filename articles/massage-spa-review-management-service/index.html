A Review Management Service for Spas: Closing the 43% Reply Gap

Personal-care businesses reply to only 57% of their reviews. The other 43% wait in silence — and a review management service exists because a spa owner's hands are on clients all day, not on a phone at the front desk.

About 68% of consumers require at least 4 stars, and 42% avoid businesses that never reply at all. Spa review management is how a small team holds that standard: massage business reviews answered daily, and a spa reputation that looks as calm and attentive as the treatment room feels.

A case we measured

A day spa with 64 Google reviews and a 4.6-star rating. Of its 10 most recent reviews, 6 had no reply at all — including a complaint about a double-booked appointment that sat unanswered while new clients kept reading it. The therapists were fully booked. The review page was the one appointment nobody kept.

A reply costs little and changes the story. To that booking complaint, a good answer is two sentences:

""We're so sorry your appointment was double-booked — your time to relax should never start with stress. Please call us and we will rebook you at the first time that suits you.""

No defensiveness — and the next client sees a spa that fixes problems instead of leaving them on display.
