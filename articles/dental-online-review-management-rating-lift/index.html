Replying is not just politeness — it changes the numbers. Online review management for dentists starts with a simple finding: once a business starts answering reviews, more reviews follow, and the rating itself tends to climb.

For dental practice reputation, the lesson is compounding: each answered review makes the next review more likely, and Google reviews for dentists slowly move in the right direction. How dentists get more reviews is rarely a trick — it is a habit, kept every week.

A case we measured

A dental practice with 118 Google reviews and a 4.3-star rating had gone quiet: of its 10 most recent reviews, 7 had no reply, including two detailed 5-star thank-yous that a new patient would read as the practice not noticing its own happiest patients.

One reply changes that read:

"Thank you for trusting us with your care — we're glad the visit went smoothly. We look forward to seeing you at your next checkup."
