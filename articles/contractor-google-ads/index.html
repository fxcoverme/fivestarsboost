Google is the platform for this trade

Emergencies are searched, not scrolled. Nobody discovers a burst pipe on TikTok, and Meta cannot target “my basement is flooding right now.” Google search — including the map results — is where high-intent home-services work is won, which is why serious PPC management for this trade starts and often stays there. Social platforms have a narrower role: planned work like remodeling or maintenance plans, where a homeowner can browse today and buy next month.

Running it yourself vs having it managed

Contractor DIY campaigns bleed in a specific way: the search terms. Job-seekers typing “hiring,” homeowners looking for do-it-yourself fixes, manufacturer brand names, and cities outside the service area all cost real money — and in this trade every wrong click is expensive, because competitors bid the same emergencies up. The other leak is timing: a 2 a.m. click that reaches voicemail is money burned, so ads should run when someone can answer the phone. Managed campaigns do the unglamorous work weekly — reading actual search terms, adding negatives, tightening the service area, tracking calls.
