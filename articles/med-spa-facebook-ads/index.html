Which platform fits a med spa

Meta (Facebook and Instagram) builds the demand: visual treatments, precisely targeted to the neighborhoods that can afford them. Its rules for health and appearance advertising are strict — no personal-attribute callouts, no sensational before-and-after framing. TikTok ads reach a younger discovery audience and suit treatment explainers. Google harvests the intent the other platforms create: “Botox near me” is typed by someone who has already decided. The working order for most med spas is Meta to create demand, Google to capture it — with review management underneath both, because the research always passes through your review profile.

Running it yourself vs having it managed

When the owner is also the injector, ads get launched between appointments and reviewed never. The leaks are predictable: targeting a whole metro for a business clients will drive twenty minutes to reach, judging campaigns by likes while the phone stays quiet, and no conversion tracking to show which ad produced a consultation. Managed campaigns hold a tight radius, write creative that follows the platform rules, track calls and consultation requests, and prune what doesn't produce them — weekly, not whenever there's a spare hour.
