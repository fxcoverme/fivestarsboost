A case we measured

A dental practice with 142 Google reviews and a 4.4-star rating. Of its 10 most recent reviews, 7 had no reply at all — including a detailed complaint about a cancelled appointment that had been sitting in public for weeks. The clinical work was not the problem. Nobody owned the review page, so every searching patient that month met the complaint first and the silence second.

A reply costs little and changes the story. To that cancellation complaint, a good answer is two sentences:

"We're sorry your appointment was cancelled at short notice — your time matters, and we should have handled it better. Please call our office and we will find you the first slot that works."

No argument, no excuses — and the next patient sees a practice that owns its mistakes instead of ignoring them.
