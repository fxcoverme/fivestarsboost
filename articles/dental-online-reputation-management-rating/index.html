Online Reputation Management for Dental Practices: What Your Rating Is Worth

75% of patients will not book a dentist rated below 4 stars. That single threshold is why online reputation management matters more for a dental practice than almost any other local business — patients decide before they ever call.

Reputation management for small business is not branding fluff here — it is appointment volume.

A case we measured

A dental practice with 96 Google reviews and a 3.9-star rating — just under the 4-star line most patients require. Of its 10 most recent reviews, 6 had no reply at all, including two detailed complaints about billing and wait times. The practice was not short on happy patients. It was short on visible answers, so the complaints defined the page.

A reply costs little and changes the story. To a billing complaint, a good answer is two sentences:

""Thank you for flagging this — billing should be clear before you leave, and we're sorry it wasn't. Please call our office and we will walk through the charges with you line by line.""

No argument, no excuses — and the next patient sees a practice that answers, instead of one sitting silently below the line.
