A case we measured

An HVAC and plumbing company with 64 Google reviews and a 4.7-star rating. Of its 10 most recent reviews, 8 had no reply at all — happy customers, thanked nowhere. The fix starts with the reply, not the request. To a recent 5-star review about a same-day water-heater swap:

"Thank you for trusting us with a same-day swap — we know a dead water heater cannot wait. We appreciate you calling us, and we are here whenever the house needs us next."

Answered reviews invite the next ones. Homeowners ask the company that visibly appreciates the customers who already wrote.
