SOCIAL MEDIA & REVIEW MANAGEMENT SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is between VelaGrid Technologies ("Provider," brand name FiveStarsBoost) and the business named below ("Client"). It covers management of Client's Google Business Profile, Facebook Page, and Instagram account.

Client business name: ______________________________

Client contact (name / phone / email): ______________________________

Start date: ______________

1. Services — what Provider delivers every month

  1. Setup (first month): claim/build and fully complete all three accounts — business hours, menu/services, photos, attributes, contact buttons, and links.
  2. Content: 8 posts per month on Facebook & Instagram and 4 posts per month on Google, from photos and information Client provides.
  3. Reviews: monitor new Google and Facebook reviews Monday–Saturday; reply to every review within 1 business day, professionally and in Client's voice.
  4. Negative and fake reviews: notify Client the same day about any negative review, with a suggested reply; report fake or policy-violating reviews to the platform for removal review.
  5. Comments & messages: answer everyday comments and common questions (hours, location, menu). Sensitive matters (Section 5) are passed to Client the same day, never answered by Provider.
  6. Review growth (white-hat only): in-store QR review cards and one review-invitation post per month.
  7. Monthly report (by the 5th): new reviews, rating trend, response rate, post performance, next month's plan.

2. Fees and payment

  • Rates follow Provider's current tier sheet: one platform $500/month; three-platform plans $799 / $999 / $1,299 / $1,599 per month depending on the service volume (posts, review coverage, reports) in the chosen tier. No separate setup fee; billing starts with the first month of service — there is no free month.
  • The $1,299 and $1,599 tiers also include a one-page starter website for Client's business (menu, hours, address, review and ordering links), built once in the first month; routine small updates are included in the content schedule.
  • Client's agreed plan (tier) and monthly fee: , due on the 1st of each month for that month's service.
  • Payment method: Card — paid through Stripe checkout on the next step.

3. Provider's responsibilities

  • Reply to every review within 1 business day; alert Client to negative reviews and sensitive messages the same day.
  • Follow each platform's rules at all times. Provider will never buy, write, or post fake reviews, and never offers incentives in exchange for positive reviews.
  • Keep Client's account access and business information confidential; use access only for the services in this Agreement.
  • Deliver the monthly report on time; promptly inform Client of anything that affects the accounts (platform changes, account warnings).

4. Provider's rights

  • If payment is over 7 days late, Provider may pause posting and replies until payment is received.
  • Provider may decline requests outside Section 1 or anything that breaks platform rules or the law (including any request for fake reviews). If Client insists on such a request, Provider may end this Agreement immediately.
  • Provider may adjust the service list or price for a future month with 30 days' written notice; Client may cancel instead of accepting the change.
  • Provider is not responsible for platform outages, platform policy changes, or platform decisions (including whether a reported review is removed).

5. Client's cooperation and responsibilities

  • One-time setup cooperation: grant Provider manager access to the three accounts, and provide any verification code sent to Client's phone during setup.
  • Provide accurate business information and materials (menu, prices, hours, photos) at setup, and notify Provider promptly when they change. Provider is not responsible for errors in information Client supplied.
  • Respond to escalations: food-safety complaints, billing disputes, catering and event inquiries, job applications, legal or media inquiries are Client's decisions. Provider passes them to Client the same day; Client responds (or tells Provider how to respond) within 1 business day.
  • Pay the monthly fee on time.
  • Client's business operations — food, service quality, pricing, staffing, licenses — remain entirely Client's responsibility. Provider manages online presence only and makes no decisions for the business itself.

6. Client's rights

  • Client owns the accounts, the content, and the data — 100%. All three accounts are registered under Client's business; Provider holds manager access only, never ownership.
  • Client may view anything Provider publishes, and may ask for any post or reply to be corrected or removed.
  • Client approves the reply style and templates at setup, and may request changes at any time.
  • Client receives the monthly report by the 5th, every month.
  • Client may cancel anytime with 30 days' written notice (see Section 8) and receive a full handover.

7. What is NOT included

  • Advertising spend or ad campaign management (available separately, in writing).
  • Website work beyond the one-page starter website included in the All-Three plan (online ordering systems, e-commerce, custom design) — quoted separately.
  • On-site photography, order taking, reservation handling.
  • Any guarantee of a specific star rating, search ranking, review removal, or sales result — platform decisions are made by Google/Meta.

8. Term and cancellation

  • Month-to-month from the start date. Either side may cancel with 30 days' written notice (email is fine). Fees are not refunded for a month already started.
  • On cancellation, Provider hands over all credentials and content files and removes its own access within 7 days.

9. Liability

Each side is responsible for its own actions. Provider's total liability under this Agreement is limited to one month's service fee (the monthly fee the Client pays under Section 2; under the founding-client price, that amount is $600). Client is responsible for the accuracy of business information published on its accounts once provided by Client.

10. Legal compliance — both sides follow U.S. law

All work under this Agreement — posts, replies, messages, and review invitations — will comply with applicable United States federal and state laws and regulations for internet marketing and online reviews, including:

  • Section 5 of the FTC Act (15 U.S.C. § 45): no unfair or deceptive practices; no false or misleading claims in any post or reply.
  • FTC Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (16 C.F.R. Part 465): no fake or bought reviews, no incentives tied to a positive (or negative) review, no undisclosed insider reviews, no suppressing or threatening reviewers, no bought followers or fake engagement. Violations carry civil penalties of up to $53,088 per violation.
  • FTC Endorsement Guides (16 C.F.R. Part 255): any endorsement, sponsored content, or material connection is clearly disclosed; AI-generated content is labeled where the platform requires it.
  • Consumer Review Fairness Act (15 U.S.C. § 45b): customers' honest reviews are protected; nothing in Client's own terms may bar or penalize them.
  • Copyright Act / DMCA (17 U.S.C.): only photos, videos, and music that Client owns or that are properly licensed are published; no content is copied from other businesses.
  • CAN-SPAM Act (15 U.S.C. §§ 7701 et seq.) and TCPA (47 U.S.C. § 227): any promotional email or text is sent only with the required consent, identification, and opt-out.
  • State privacy laws (e.g., CCPA/CPRA where applicable): customer information received through messages is used only to operate Client's accounts and is never sold.
  • Platform rules: Google and Meta terms and content policies are followed at all times (Google additionally bans any incentive for reviews).

Client confirms the materials and information it provides are accurate and lawful and do not infringe anyone's rights. Neither side may ask the other to break these rules; if a law or platform rule changes, both sides will cooperate to adjust the work.
