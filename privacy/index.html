Privacy Policy

FiveStarsBoost mobile app and web tool · Effective November 5, 2026

VelaGrid Technologies ("we", "us") operates FiveStarsBoost. This policy explains what information the FiveStarsBoost mobile app and web tool handle. The short version: the app works on your device. Your reviews, replies, templates and business details stay on your phone — we never receive them.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores three things, only on your device:

  • Your business details (name, phone, hours) that you type in, used to personalize your replies.
  • Your saved reply templates.
  • A monthly count of replies you have generated, used to apply the free-plan limit.

Reply generation happens entirely on your device. The review text you paste is not sent to us or to any server.

What we collect

From the app: nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising trackers. We cannot see your reviews, your replies, or your business details.

On our website, if you choose to submit a form (for example the free review checkup, a registration, or a service agreement), we use the information you enter only to respond to you and provide the service. We do not sell personal information.

Payments

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple, Google, or Stripe, depending on where you buy. We never see or store your card number.

Deleting your data

In the app, use "Delete my data" on the Plan screen — it permanently removes everything the app has stored on your device. Uninstalling the app also removes this data. For information you submitted through a website form, email us at info@fivestarsboost.com and we will delete it.

Children

FiveStarsBoost is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information — for example, if a future version adds accounts or cloud features — we will update this page and note the new effective date before the change takes effect.
